FAQ

Can this work on a small porch?

Yes. Keep the same design logic but reduce the number of containers.

Should I use real or faux plants?

Use real plants for focal points and faux for difficult areas or maintenance reduction.

Which concept is best for most homes?

Concept B is the best default because it balances visual impact, cost, and maintenance.
