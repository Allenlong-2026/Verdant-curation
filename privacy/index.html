Privacy Policy

How we handle your data.

Last updated: June 9, 2026

Verdant Curation ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website.

Information you provide voluntarily: When you subscribe to our newsletter or contact us via email, we collect your name and email address.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use minimal cookies for basic functionality. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

4. Third-Party Services

Our site may contain links to third-party websites (Amazon, Pinterest, etc.). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. Amazon affiliate links are disclosed in accordance with FTC guidelines.

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to any personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@verdantcuration.com.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
