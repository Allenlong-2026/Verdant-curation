About Verdant Curation

Evidence-based plant design guides for greener homes and happier spaces.

Verdant Curation was created for anyone who believes a home feels different when plants are part of it. We're here to help you make that happen — one container, one porch, one green corner at a time.

Our approach

Most plant content is either too shallow ("buy this and put it in a pot") or too academic for practical use. We sit in the middle — evidence-based guides that you can actually execute.

Every guide starts with real plants, matched to real environments. We tell you which species work together, why they work, and how to care for them. The theory is there when you want it, but the step-by-step is front and center.

Why Verdant Curation

Because plant information should be practical, beautiful, and trustworthy. We don't do vague recommendations. Every plant, every pot size, every care tip has been verified against reliable horticultural sources. We cite what we know and label what we're testing.

This site is AI-assisted content with a strong editorial filter — every guide is researched, structured, and reviewed before publication. We prioritize accuracy over volume, and depth over breadth.
