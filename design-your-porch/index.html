Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get my design? +

We send your personalized design within 24 hours. Most designs arrive in 12–18 hours. You’ll receive an email with a preview and download link.

What if I don’t like the design? +

No problem — it’s completely free. You can request a revision with different plants or layout, or try a different style. No commitment, no strings attached.

Do you sell the plants shown in the design? +

Yes, many of the featured plants are available as high-quality faux options from our shop. Your design includes a shopping guide showing which plants you can buy from us and which you can find locally.
